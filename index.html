Chapter III • Front-Line Source Intake

The Real-World Source Material Crisis

In real litigation and forensic research, documents do not arrive cataloged, OCR-indexed, or clean. Pro se litigants, public defenders, and legal aid clinics face fragmented digital debris: generic filenames, duplicate servicer portal dumps, unindexed archives, and missing manifests. Executive Function OS was built from the ground up to solve this front-line intake barrier.

⚠ The Front-Line Reality

Unmanifested Digital Chaos

  • ×
    Generic Auto-Saved Names: Files saved as download (3).pdf, scan0022.pdf, Untitled.pdf, or 174.pdf, completely obscuring their evidentiary provenance.
  • ×
    Multi-Servicer Portal Dumps: Portals (Shellpoint, Countrywide, Bank of America) generate duplicate monthly statements, contradictory payoff letters, and redundant transaction histories.
  • ×
    Inaccessible Container Formats: Multi-page unindexed TIFFs, monolithic court files, and nested zip/tar archives that cannot be searched on standard mobile devices.
  • ×
    No Verifiable Manifest: No central index, no cryptographic hashes, no page maps, and no cross-references connecting documents to court dockets.
✓ The EFOS Ingestion Solution

The Automated Manifest Engine

  • ✓
    Macro-Disaggregation (DocSplit): Opens compressed archives in memory in <70ms, decomposes multi-page filings, and runs high-fidelity client-side OCR without cloud leakage.
  • ✓
    Cross-Servicer Deduplication: Automatically detects duplicate statements, normalizes payment histories, and isolates genuine chain-of-title defects.
  • ✓
    Semantic Re-Indexing: Transforms opaque strings like Loan_AvailableDocument_1772594860914.pdf into transparent legal assets like 2016-04-12 Garboden Bankruptcy Trustee Settlement Notice.
  • ✓
    Cryptographic Case Manifests: Compiles comprehensive indices (MASTER_TEXT_CORPUS_MANIFEST.json) with SHA-256 hashes, page ranges, and direct exhibit links.

Chapter IV • Core Methodology

Dual Core Engines: Percolation Phenotyping & Legal Provenance

Executive Function OS bridges computational psychiatry with adversarial document intelligence. Two parallel, client-side pipelines transform raw behavioral traces into quantitative phenotypes, and unindexed legal corpora into decision-grade court pleadings.

ENGINE A

Percolation Dynamics & Cognitive Fragmentation Pipeline (Bioinformatics)

STAGE 01

Passive Trace Ingestion

Ingests naturalistic digital activity (Google Takeout, Drive Activity API, Docs edit logs) client-side into volatile RAM without uploading sensitive behavioral traces.

STAGE 02

Entropy Vectorization

Discretizes event streams into sliding temporal windows to extract Shannon document entropy, inter-event latency, and task-switching velocity.

STAGE 03

DBSCAN Mode Discovery

Identifies natural cognitive manifolds (sustained synthesis vs. hyper-switching context churn) while isolating unclustered executive stalls as noise.

STAGE 04

Phase Breakdown Simulation

Models bond dilution under deadline stress, estimates critical threshold (p_c = 0.42), and quantifies Observer–Operator decoupling lag.

ENGINE B

Multi-Agent Legal Provenance & Synthesis Pipeline (Justice & Forensics)

STAGE 01

Corpus Ingestion & OCR

Raw, unstructured PDFs, transcripts, court dockets, and audio records pass through client-side OCR and extraction pipelines without external data exfiltration.

STAGE 02

Entity Knowledge Graph

Chronological timelines, relational actors, statutory duties, and exhibits are structured into an audited graph network with explicit bi-directional links.

STAGE 03

Adversarial Auditing

Every factual proposition is cross-referenced against original exhibits. Citations are verified live via CourtListener. Hallucinated quotes and fabricated rules are purged.

STAGE 04

Decision-Grade Drafts

Clean, formatted deliverables compiled in locked 28-line UTCR pleading format or peer-reviewable research reports ready for immediate external scrutiny.
