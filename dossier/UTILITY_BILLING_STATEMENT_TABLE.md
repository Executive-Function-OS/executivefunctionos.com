## 2. Evidentiary Audit: Verified Facts vs. Unverified Hypotheses

### A. Verified Primary Facts (In Documented Record)
1. **$150/Month Plan Compliance:** Monthly payments of $150.00 were remitted on time under protest from March through October 2026.
2. **Deceptive Billing & Coercion:** On every bill from May through October 2026, the City printed a statement amount due exceeding $3,700–$4,000, embedding the $2,438.69 labor surcharge into the total, and accompanied each bill with a red-letter Shut Off Notice threatening immediate water disconnection.
