## PLAINTIFF'S OBJECTION TO FORM OF PROPOSED LIMITED JUDGMENT AND LIMITED OBJECTION TO FORM OF PROPOSED ORDER (UTCR 5.100)

Plaintiff Annika Eriksson, appearing pro se, respectfully objects under UTCR 5.100 to the form of the proposed "Limited Judgment of Dismissal of Plaintiff's Claims With Prejudice" and makes a limited objection to the proposed "Order on Plaintiff's, Donald Buckhout's, and Defendant City of Oregon City's Motions," both submitted on or about December 22, 2025.

### I. PROCEDURAL BACKGROUND

1. On June 24, 2025, the bank-related defendants (The Bank of New York Mellon in its OC10 and OC11 trustee capacities and NewRez LLC d/b/a Shellpoint Mortgage Servicing, collectively the "Financial Defendants") filed "DEFENDANTS' MOTION TO DISMISS AMENDED COMPLAINT" pursuant to ORCP 21. That motion was brought only on behalf of the Financial Defendants and sought dismissal of Plaintiff's Amended Complaint as to them. The City of Oregon City was not a moving party on that motion.

2. On September 3, 2025, the Court granted the Financial Defendants' Motion to Dismiss Amended Complaint. The corresponding order, submitted by counsel for the Financial Defendants, is titled "[PROPOSED] ORDER GRANTING DEFENDANTS' MOTION TO DISMISS AMENDED COMPLAINT" and recites that the matter came before the Court on the motion of BONYM-OC10, BONYM-OC11, and NewRez, LLC. It then states that "Defendants' Motion to Dismiss is GRANTED. Plaintiff's claims are hereby dismissed with prejudice." That order was entered on the register on or about September 17, 2025.

3. On October 31, 2025, after that order was entered, Plaintiff emailed City's counsel asserting that, in her view, discovery should be stayed pending her ORCP 71 motion. City's counsel contemporaneously took the position that Plaintiff's discovery obligations remained outstanding and that discovery between Plaintiff and the City should go forward.

4. Separately, the City later filed its own Motion to Dismiss, Motion to Compel, and Motion to Strike proposed intervenor Donald Buckhout's Motion to Intervene. On December 8, 2025, the Court held a hearing on those motions and on Plaintiff's Motion to Set Aside the September 17 dismissal. Following the hearing, counsel for the City submitted a proposed "Order on Plaintiff's, Donald Buckhout's, and Defendant City of Oregon City's Motions" which, if signed, would deny Plaintiff's Motion to Set Aside, deny Buckhout's Motion to Intervene as moot, and deny the City's Motion to Dismiss, Motion to Compel, and Motion to Strike as moot. The Court has never entered any order granting the City's Motion to Dismiss on the merits.

### I I. OBJECTION TO PROPOSED LIMITED JUDGMENT

The proposed Limited Judgment states that "judgment is entered dismissing all of plaintiff's claims against all defendants with prejudice." This language does not accurately reflect the Court's rulings.

As described above, on September 3, 2025, the Court granted the Financial Defendants' Motion to Dismiss Plaintiff's Amended Complaint with prejudice. That order was entered on the register on September 17, 2025 and arose solely from the Financial Defendants' ORCP 21 motion and their proposed order. On December 8, 2025, the Court denied Plaintiff's Motion to Set Aside that dismissal and denied the City of Oregon City's Motion to Dismiss, Motion to Compel, and Motion to Strike as moot.

The Court has never entered an order granting the City's Motion to Dismiss on the merits. By stating "against all defendants," the proposed Limited Judgment improperly converts the City's moot denial into a new, merits-based dismissal with prejudice. This creates ambiguity regarding which claims and parties were actually adjudicated, on what date, and the scope of any appeal.

Plaintiff respectfully requests that the Court decline to sign the proposed Limited Judgment and instead direct that any final judgment:

1. Expressly identify the September 3, 2025 order as the basis for dismissal of claims against the Financial Defendants;

2. Note that Plaintiff's Motion to Set Aside was denied on December 8, 2025; and

3. Remove or modify "against all defendants" to avoid entering a new merits dismissal of claims against the City, whose motions were denied as moot.

### I II. LIMITED OBJECTION TO PROPOSED ORDER

Plaintiff does not contest the portions of the proposed Order denying the City's Motion to Dismiss, Motion to Compel, and Motion to Strike as moot, nor the denial of Donald Buckhout's Motion to Intervene as moot.

Plaintiff objects solely to the inclusion of "with prejudice" in the phrase: "Plaintiff's Motion to Set Aside Dismissal is DENIED with prejudice."

Plaintiff appeared remotely at the December 8, 2025 hearing while acutely ill and has ordered the official audio recording. Plaintiff does not clearly recall the Court expressly stating "with prejudice" when denying the Motion to Set Aside. The audio may show that this phrase does not conform to the actual oral ruling.

Plaintiff respectfully requests that the Court either strike "with prejudice" so the order reads "Plaintiff's Motion to Set Aside Dismissal is DENIED," or decline to include that language unless the hearing record confirms the Court used those specific words.

Plaintiff does not seek to re-argue the merits, but only to ensure the written Order accurately reflects the Court's December 8, 2025 oral ruling.
