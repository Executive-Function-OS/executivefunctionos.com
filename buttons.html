Drop-In Integration Guide

Pure zero-dependency CSS. Fully compatible with Tailwind CSS, React, Vue, Svelte, or vanilla HTML.

STEP 1: LINK STYLESHEET IN YOUR <HEAD>
<!-- Import Canva AI Tokens & Kinetic Clarity Buttons --> <link rel="stylesheet" href="tokens.css"> <link rel="stylesheet" href="kinetic_clarity_buttons.css">
STEP 2: ADD CLASSES TO ANY <button> OR <a> ELEMENT
<!-- Primary Kinetic Shimmer CTA --> <button class="sc-btn sc-kinetic-shimmer sc-btn--lg"> Execute Mission </button> <!-- HUD Technical Bracket Link --> <a href="/telemetry" class="sc-btn sc-outline-bracket"> [VIEW_TELEMETRY] </a>
ACCESSIBILITY & REDUCED MOTION BUILT-IN

All kinetic animations automatically disable when the user has enabled prefers-reduced-motion: reduce in their operating system. Focus outlines strictly adhere to WCAG AAA contrast requirements against both dark slate and light backgrounds.
